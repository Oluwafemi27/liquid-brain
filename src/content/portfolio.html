AI automations that keep running

  1. TriggerA message, email or form arrives
  2. CleanData is checked and shaped
  3. ThinkAn AI step answers, extracts or summarizes
  4. Hand offResults land in the tools people use

Neon Chess

Live

A chess arena you can play against an AI opponent, in a holographic 3D scene.

What I built

A match lobby, live clocks, move history and in-game chat around a Three.js board, so a game feels like a real match and not a demo.

How it works

React and TypeScript drive the interface and game state. Three.js renders the board and pieces. Tailwind styles the lobby and panels.
